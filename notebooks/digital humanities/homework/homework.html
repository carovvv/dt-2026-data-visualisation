Italy has the largest number of heritage sites in this dataset, followed by the United Kingdom, France, Belgium, and Austria. Some countries, such as Croatia and Czechia, have only one site represented. This shows that the dataset is not evenly distributed across countries, with some countries having a much larger representation of heritage sites.

plt.ylabel("Area (hectares)")

plt.show()

The scatter plot does not show a clear relationship between the year a site was inscribed and its area. Large and small heritage sites were added to the World Heritage List throughout the different years. There are several particularly large sites, but they do not appear to be concentrated in one specific period.

Medieval sites are the most common historical period in this dataset. Several sites also combine multiple periods, such as Medieval–Modern or Medieval–Renaissance. This suggests that many of the heritage sites represent long periods of historical development rather than belonging to only one specific period.
